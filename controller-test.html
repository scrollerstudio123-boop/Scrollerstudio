What to check when a controller fails this test

No controller detected

Chrome and most browsers only register a gamepad after you press a button on it — simply plugging it in isn't enough to trigger detection.

A button never lights up

Confirm it isn't a driver-level remap first (common on third-party controllers), then suspect a worn contact under that button if other buttons work fine.

Stick doesn't return to center

A dot that rests away from the middle when you let go points to stick drift — a very common failure mode on aging analog sticks.

Wireless input lag or dropout

Try a wired connection or fresh batteries to rule out a weak signal before assuming a hardware fault.

Frequently asked questions

Why do I need to press a button before anything shows up?

Browsers require a user gesture on the controller itself before exposing it through the Gamepad API — this is a privacy and security measure, not a bug.

Does this work with PlayStation, Xbox, and Switch controllers?

Most modern controllers work over USB or Bluetooth once paired at the OS level, though button numbering can vary slightly by controller brand and browser.

Why does my controller show up but only some inputs register?

Some controllers require a specific USB mode (DirectInput vs XInput) to expose all buttons and triggers correctly — check your controller's manual or companion app if triggers or extra buttons don't respond.
