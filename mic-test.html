Why a mic can appear connected but not work

Wrong input selected

Your OS or browser may default to a different microphone than the one you expect — check your system sound settings if the trace stays flat.

Muted at the hardware level

Many headsets and USB mics have a physical mute button or LED indicator — worth checking before assuming a software fault.

Permission blocked

If your browser previously denied mic access for this site, you'll need to reset that permission in your browser's site settings before it can request access again.

Level too low

A faint but present trace usually means gain is just turned down — check your system input volume before suspecting a hardware fault.

Frequently asked questions

Is my audio recorded or sent anywhere?

No. The waveform is drawn directly from your microphone's live signal using the Web Audio API — nothing is recorded, saved, or transmitted.

Why does the browser ask for permission every time?

Microphone access is granted per-site and, in some browsers, per-session for privacy reasons. You can make it persistent from your browser's site settings if you'd rather not be asked again.

The waveform is jumpy or cuts out — is that normal?

Brief flickers can happen from background noise processing your OS applies automatically. A mic that cuts out completely and repeatedly is more likely a loose connection or driver issue.
