Common webcam problems this can help catch

SymptomLikely cause
Camera list shows nothingAnother app (a video call already open, or a security suite) may be holding exclusive access to the camera.
Feed is blackA physical privacy shutter or lens cover, or a driver that connected but isn't streaming frames.
Very low resolutionSome browsers request a low default resolution unless a site explicitly asks for a higher one — this doesn't necessarily mean the hardware is limited.
Choppy or low frame rateOften a USB bandwidth or lighting issue — many webcams automatically drop frame rate in low light to keep the image brighter.

Frequently asked questions

Is my video recorded or sent anywhere?

No. The preview streams directly from your camera to the video element on this page — nothing is recorded, saved, or transmitted anywhere.

Why does it say my camera is in use by another app?

Most cameras only allow one application to access them at a time. Close other video-calling apps or browser tabs using the camera and try again.

Can I test a second, external webcam?

If your system has multiple cameras connected, your browser's permission prompt or site settings usually let you choose which one to use — check there if the wrong camera appears.
